How to play

  • Jump: tap anywhere on the screen. Hold your finger down to jump higher; let go early for a short hop.
  • Slide: hold the Slide button in the bottom-left corner to slide under low branches and birds. Let go to stand back up.
  • Pause: use the pause button at the top of the screen. The speaker button turns game sound on or off.
  • Keyboard (iPad with a keyboard attached): Space, ↑ or W to jump, ↓ or S to slide.

Jump over hydrants and other things on the sidewalk, slide under what hangs low, and see how many miles the car takes you.

The road

  • Speed: the car slowly speeds up the farther you go, with busy stretches followed by calmer ones so you can catch your breath.
  • Stop signs bring the car to a full stop, school zones slow it down, and highway stretches speed it up. Watch for the road-sign alerts at the top of the screen.
  • The route changes every mile: the suburbs, farm country, the city, the desert and the mountains, and then around again.

Bandages and continues

The bandage on your finger is an extra life: with it, you can take one hit and keep running. If you lose it, keep an eye out for a new bandage on the road.

If you crash without a bandage and have enough snacks, you get a few seconds to continue by spending them. The first continue in a run costs 25 snacks, and each extra continue in the same run costs twice as much as the one before.

Snacks, missions and levels

  • Snacks are the in-game currency. Grab them along the road; they are added to your total at the end of each run.
  • You always have three missions, such as “Jump 4 fire hydrants in one run” or “Slide under 5 birds or branches in one run”. Each finished mission pays out snacks and is replaced by a new one.
  • Every three missions you finish raises your level, and each level gives you a bigger snack bonus on every run.

The shop

Spend your snacks in the shop on 25 hand styles, sleeves, cars and power-ups. Everything in the shop is bought with snacks you earn by playing. There are no real-money purchases and no ads.

Power-ups

  • Road RageThe car floors it to 100 mph for 10 seconds and you smash straight through obstacles.Tap its button during a run. One per run.
  • Sugar RushFor 15 seconds, snacks and plates fly to you, and every snack counts double.Tap its button during a run.
  • Spare BandageStart a run with two bandages, and carry up to two for the whole run.Used automatically when your next run starts.
  • Plate RadarFor one run, rare plates start showing up twice as early and three times as often.Used automatically when your next run starts.

Spare Bandage and Plate Radar can be switched on or off before a run with the Next run buttons on the start screen, so you can save them for later.

License plates

Plates from all 50 U.S. states float along the road, often above obstacles or under branches. Collect each state once to fill your collection; tap Plates to see which ones you have.

Most states are common, but rarer plates only start turning up deeper into a run, and the farther you go the better your chances. Alaska and Hawaii are legendary: they only appear after many miles in a single run, and even then they are a long shot.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your snacks, level, missions, shop items, power-ups, plates and best distance are saved automatically on your device as you play, and they are kept when you update the app.

Can I move my progress to another iPhone or iPad?

There are no accounts and no online sync, so progress does not move between devices on its own. If you set up a new device from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, your progress normally comes along with the backup.

How do I reset my progress and start over?

There is no reset button in the game, so a fresh start is never one accidental tap away. To start over:

  1. Touch and hold the Backseat Runner icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App.
  2. Reinstall Backseat Runner from the App Store.

This permanently erases all of your progress, including snacks, items and plates. Nothing is stored online, so it cannot be recovered afterwards. (“Offload App” does not reset anything: it keeps your game data.)

I lost my progress. Can you restore it?

Sorry, no. Your progress exists only on your device, and I never receive a copy. If you had a device backup from before the progress was lost, restoring that backup may bring it back.

I can’t find Alaska or Hawaii. Are they really in the game?

Yes. They are the rarest plates and only appear far into a single run. Your best chances: go as far as you can without crashing, use Plate Radar so rare plates turn up earlier and more often, keep Spare Bandages handy to survive longer, and use Sugar Rush to pull in plates that are hard to reach.

How do power-ups work?

Buy them in the shop with snacks. Road Rage and Sugar Rush appear as buttons during a run; tap one to use it. Spare Bandage and Plate Radar are used automatically at the start of your next run, unless you switch them off with the Next run buttons on the start screen. See Power-ups above.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game runs completely offline, so it is perfect for the car, a plane or anywhere without signal.

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. Snacks are earned only by playing and cannot be bought with real money, and there are no ads.

Does the app collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics or tracking, and the app never connects to the internet. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I can’t hear any sound.

Check that the speaker button in the game is not muted, that the volume is up, and that your device’s silent mode is off: game sounds follow the silent switch. Game sounds play alongside your own music instead of stopping it.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. The game plays in landscape, so turn your device sideways.
