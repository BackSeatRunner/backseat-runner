Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version: Backseat Runner does not collect any information about you.

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, ads or tracking
  • No third-party services or SDKs
  • No internet connection used: the game runs fully offline
  • Your game progress is saved only on your own device

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Backseat Runner app for iPhone and iPad, made by Omayya Harvey Atout, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). It explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information the app collects

None. Backseat Runner does not collect, send, share or sell any personal information or usage data. In particular, the app:

  • has no accounts, sign-in, chat or social features;
  • contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs;
  • makes no network requests: the whole game is bundled inside the app and works in airplane mode;
  • does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other permission;
  • does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the app saves a small amount of game data in its own private storage on your device (iOS app storage, also known as UserDefaults). This includes:

  • your snack balance, level and current missions;
  • the hands, sleeves, cars and power-ups you own or have equipped;
  • the license plates you have collected and your best distance;
  • whether game sound is muted.

This data never leaves your device through the app, and I have no way to see it. It does not include your name or anything else that identifies you.

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS may include this game data in those backups, as it does for most apps. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; I do not have access to them.

Purchases and advertising

There are no in-app purchases and no ads. Snacks are an in-game currency that you earn only by playing, and they cannot be bought with real money.

Third parties

The app does not share any information with third parties, because it does not collect any.

The app is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple may give developers general, anonymous App Store statistics (such as download counts), and crash reports only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings. None of this identifies you to me, and it is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

Backseat Runner is made to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). This is consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. The app has no ads, no purchases with real money, no chat and no links that lead out of the game.

Parents and guardians: there is no information about your child held by me to review or delete. All game progress stays on the device and can be removed by deleting the app.

How to delete your data

All of the app’s data is on your device, so you are fully in control of it. To delete it, delete the app:

  • Touch and hold the Backseat Runner icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App; or
  • Go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage), choose Backseat Runner and tap Delete App.

This permanently erases your saved progress from the device. (“Offload App” is different: it keeps your game data.) A copy may remain inside existing device backups until those backups are replaced or deleted.

Since I hold no data about you, there is nothing for you to request from me, but you are always welcome to contact me with questions. This also means the rights given by laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, like access, correction or deletion, have no data on my side to apply to.

Security

Your game data stays in the app’s private storage on your device, protected by iOS. Nothing is sent over the internet, so there is nothing to intercept.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information, I will update this page and the date at the top before or when that version is released, and update the app’s privacy details on the App Store to match. Please check this page from time to time.
